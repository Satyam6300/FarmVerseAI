How to read vegetation indices

What they are. NDVI and EVI compare red and near-infrared light reflected by the surface. Dense green vegetation reflects much more near-infrared, so values rise as plants grow.

What they are not. They do not measure yield, nutrients or crop health directly. Bare soil, cloud residue, weeds and trees all change the value.

This data. MODIS Terra 16-day composites, about 250 m, read from NASA GIBS tiles through the official colormap (NDVI classes 0.01 wide, EVI 0.025). The field value is one pixel of about 300 m.

SMAP surface soil moisture, district mean

SMAP L4

SMAP Level 4 analysed surface soil moisture (top 5 cm), 9 km, 12:00 UTC, sampled every 16 days from April 2015. Level 4 combines SMAP radiometer observations with a land surface model. The record starts in 2015, so it cannot show long-term change.

NASA POWER modelled soil wetness at the pilot point

Model (MERRA-2)

Monthly mean of GWETTOP (surface layer) and GWETROOT (root zone), dimensionless, 0 = dry and 1 = saturated. Model output, shown separately from SMAP and never mixed with sensor readings.

Field sensor soil moisture

Ground sensors measure at one depth at one spot and in their own units. They are shown on the IoT Monitoring page and are not combined with satellite or model values here.
