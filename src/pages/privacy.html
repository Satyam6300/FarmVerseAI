Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy describes how the current version of the site works. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and does not claim compliance with any specific law.

1. Summary

FARMVERSE AI has no user accounts, no cookies, no advertising and no analytics. It has no FARMVERSE server that receives your entries. Your farm profile is stored in your own browser. Some features send limited information to third-party services, listed in section 6.

2. Information the site handles

  • Farm profile (My Farm): farm name, village, district, location, field outline, area, crops, soil details, irrigation and water availability. All optional.
  • Settings: language, Farmer or Expert view, and the address of a sensor backend if you add one.
  • Digital Twin scenarios and the guided demo step.
  • Technical data: the host serving these files and any third-party service you use receive your IP address and browser details, as with any website.

The site does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, land record number or any government identifier. Please do not enter them.

3. Farm locations and field outlines

If you place your farm or draw a field, the coordinates are saved in your browser's localStorage. They leave your device only when a feature needs them: Climate Analytics sends the coordinates you choose to NASA POWER, and map views request NASA and basemap tiles for the area on screen.

4. Sensor readings

No sensor readings are collected by this site. If you connect a sensor backend in Settings, the site reads readings from that backend, which you or your team operate. The demo mode on the IoT page generates simulated values in the browser and sends nothing.

5. How data is stored and used

The farm profile and settings stay in localStorage until you delete them. Scenarios and the demo step are in sessionStorage, which the browser clears when the tab closes. Your entries are used only to show you charts, comparisons and reports. They are not sold, shared or used to train any model.

6. Third-party services

  • NASA POWER (power.larc.nasa.gov): receives coordinates and dates when climate data is requested live.
  • NASA GIBS (gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov): receives requests for satellite map tiles of the area you view.
  • Esri World Imagery and OpenStreetMap tile servers: receive requests for basemap tiles of the area you view.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: receives the text you type into place search.
  • Your sensor backend, if configured.
  • Hosting provider: to be named before launch. It may keep standard server logs.

Each service's own privacy policy applies. Fonts, icons and charting code are served from this site.

7. Retention

FARMVERSE AI keeps nothing on a server. Browser storage remains until you delete it or clear site data.

8. Security

  • HTTPS will be required once the site is launched on its custom domain.
  • A Content Security Policy limits scripts to this site and network requests to the services listed above.
  • No API keys or secrets are included in the site's code. The sensor backend requires its own key to accept readings.

9. Your choices

Every feature works without personal information. You can delete the stored profile and settings under Settings, or by clearing site data in your browser.

10. Changes

If the site starts handling information differently, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
