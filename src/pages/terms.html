1. The service

FARMVERSE AI is a free website built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. It shows climate, vegetation and soil-moisture information from NASA sources, district crop statistics and guidance from an ICAR-CRIDA contingency plan, and lets users compare crop rotations. It is a prototype and may change, break or be withdrawn at any time. By using it you accept these terms.

2. Your responsibilities

  • Enter farm information as accurately as you can; comparisons depend on it.
  • Do not enter personal information about yourself or others.
  • Do not disrupt the site or use it to send excessive automated requests to NASA or other services.
  • Check the linked sources before relying on any figure.

3. Data accuracy

Climate values describe a grid of about 50 km; satellite values are read from visualisation tiles and quantised; crop statistics are district averages; agronomic content comes from a 2011 plan. Values are processed by code that may contain errors. When data is missing, the site says so instead of estimating.

4. Agricultural decision-support disclaimer

FARMVERSE AI provides information to support discussion. It is not agronomic, financial or professional advice and does not replace an agricultural officer, Krishi Vigyan Kendra scientist or other qualified adviser. The decision about what to plant rests with the farmer. The site does not account for pests, seed availability, markets, credit, labour or many other factors.
