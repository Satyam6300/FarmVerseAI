How the advisor works

  • It matches your question to a topic and builds the answer from data already loaded in the app: NASA POWER, MODIS, SMAP, district crop statistics, the ICAR-CRIDA plan and your farm profile.
  • It does not use a large language model and does not invent figures, citations or advice.
  • It never gives fertiliser, pesticide or irrigation doses.
  • Every answer labels observations, model estimates and simulated scenarios.
