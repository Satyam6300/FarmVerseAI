About FARMVERSE AI

A decision-support tool for one question: which crop rotation a rainfed farm in Kalaburagi should consider for its next growing cycle.

The problem

Most farmland in Kalaburagi is rainfed. The ICAR-CRIDA contingency plan for the district records 974.1 thousand hectares of rainfed area against 182.6 thousand hectares net irrigated, and lists drought as a regular contingency. Rotation choices based on how seasons used to behave may not suit the rainfall a farm receives now.

The challenge

Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data asks teams to build decision-support tools that pair NASA Earth observations with local farming data. FARMVERSE AI combines NASA observations, local soil information, crop characteristics and farmer priorities to help farmers explore rotation strategies.

What it does today

Evidence

  • 35 years of NASA POWER daily climate at the pilot point.
  • MODIS NDVI and EVI since 2001 and SMAP soil moisture since 2015, decoded from NASA GIBS.
  • District crop statistics from 1997-98 to 2024-25.

Decisions

  • Rotation comparison and shortlist from the ICAR-CRIDA district plan.
  • A Farm Digital Twin that tests crop calendars against observed weather.
  • A rule-based advisor that cites the evidence and lists what is missing.

Honesty

  • A baseline yield model, validated year by year and shown even where it fails to beat a trend line.
  • Every value tagged as observed, model estimate, simulated, farmer entered, demo or missing.
  • Works offline with cached data.

Not yet connected

  • The team's Sentinel-2 and Earth Engine yield pipeline (an import slot is ready).
  • The team's LoRa sensors (the backend and MQTT bridge are written and tested; the hardware is not connected).
  • Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 field-scale imagery.
