Data sources and limitations

  • Climate: NASA POWER daily data (MERRA-2 based, about 50 km grid) at 17.33 N, 76.83 E. Not a field measurement.
  • Vegetation: MODIS Terra 16-day NDVI from NASA GIBS, about 250 m. NDVI is not a measure of crop health or nutrients.
  • Soil moisture: SMAP Level 4 surface (top 5 cm), 9 km, model value-added, since 2015.
  • Agronomy: ICAR-CRIDA Agriculture Contingency Plan for Gulbarga district (2011). Crop durations: published variety lists.
  • Scenarios are calendars tested against past weather. They are not yield, income, soil-health or water-saving predictions.
  • This report supports discussion with an agricultural officer. It is not advice.
