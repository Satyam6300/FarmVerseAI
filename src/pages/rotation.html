    The rules, in full

    • Listed for this soil: the ICAR-CRIDA Gulbarga plan names the system as a normal system for the selected soil situation.
    • Legume: any crop in the sequence is a legume (pigeonpea, green gram, black gram, soybean, chickpea). Green manure depends on species.
    • Climate evidence: NASA POWER means for 1991 to 2010 against 2011 onward at the pilot point.
    • No shortlist without a soil situation. Rotations are never scored or ranked.

    Before acting on this

    • The catalogue is the 10 rainfed systems of one district plan published in 2011.
    • District yields are historical averages, not farm forecasts.
    • Profit, prices, inputs and nitrogen quantities are not estimated.
    • Discuss any change with the Krishi Vigyan Kendra or an agricultural officer.
