How it works

From satellite record to a rotation you can discuss

01

Set up the farm

Place the field on the map, add crops, soil and water. Unknown details stay marked as unknown.

02

Read the evidence

Rainfall, dry spells, heat, vegetation and soil moisture, each with source, date and resolution.

03

Simulate rotations

Three crop calendars on one field, tested against every year of observed weather.

04

Take it to an expert

Print a report with evidence, trade-offs and missing information for the agricultural officer.

Farm Digital Twin

What changes if the farmer chooses this rotation?

Change a crop, sowing date or duration and watch the field, the season calendar and the comparison update. The twin reports rain during each crop window, the chance of a two-week dry spell after sowing, heat days and months of soil cover.

It does not invent yields, profits or soil improvements. Simulated rows are tagged as simulated.

Scientific limits

What FARMVERSE AI does not claim

NASA POWER values describe a grid cell of about 50 km, not one field. MODIS pixels are about 250 m.
NDVI measures greenness in a composite, not crop health or nutrients.
The baseline yield model is tested year by year and shown even where it does not beat a simple trend.
District yields are government averages, not predictions for a farm.
Rotations come from one 2011 district plan for rainfed farms and are never ranked.
Field sensors are not connected yet; the IoT page has a clearly labelled demo mode.
