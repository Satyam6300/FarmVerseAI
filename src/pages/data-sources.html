Known limitations

  • NASA POWER precipitation is MERRA-2 bias-corrected precipitation (reanalysis), on a grid of about 0.5 degree. One value represents roughly 50 km.
  • Missing days (fill value -999) are excluded. A season with any missing day is left out of averages, never filled.
  • MODIS and SMAP values are decoded from NASA GIBS visualisation tiles using the official colormaps, so they are quantised to colormap classes (NDVI 0.01, EVI 0.025, SMAP 0.003 m³/m³) and lower zoom levels are resampled.
  • SMAP Level 4 is model value-added and starts in 2015.
  • District crop statistics are as reported by the government; abrupt changes in area can reflect reporting or boundary changes (Yadgir formed in 2009-10). Rows with area below 10% of the median are flagged.
  • The ICAR-CRIDA plan dates from 2011 and covers rainfed farming. Its crop table refers to the district as it was then.
  • Crop durations come from variety lists; most are from Tamil Nadu Agricultural University pages and may differ from Karnataka varieties.
  • The demonstration field is an illustrative outline drawn on satellite imagery, not a surveyed parcel.
